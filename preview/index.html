How breeding works

Scroll through one egg, from first date to first steps. The pixels are real: the page runs the game's own genetics.

    Meet the parents

    Parent A is your own Friend, checked in your wallet. Here it is Friend #77949, a Cellular. Parent B is a wild Friend from the chain: 73 of them, 8 from every family.

    Love at first pixel

    The Matchmaker offers three wild Friends at a time. New faces are free. Pick one, and the two of them get along very well.

    One egg, one of a kind

    An egg costs 1 RF in the game (simulated). Its seed comes from both parents and the play, so the same pair lays a new baby every time.

    Sixteen rows, two parents

    A Friend is 16 rows of 16 pixels. The baby takes each row from A or B, in runs of 2 to 5 rows, at least 4 from each parent. Pixels are only ever added to keep one body, never removed.

    In the Gene Lab you can lock rows to a parent before the egg: the ledger rolls the rarity, you design the genes.

    From #77949From #190688

    It walks like both

    The same row mask runs through all 64 frames, idle and walk, in four directions. So the baby's walk is a real mix of both parents' walks.

    The roll decides the look

    Every egg rolls a tier. Patterns and new shapes grow on top of the inherited rows, and the rarest coat is a rainbow.

    Shapes run in the family

    A Mutant's horns live in its own pixel rows. Breed it again, and a baby that takes those rows carries them, even as a Common. Measured: 53.7% (231 of 430 in the tests), about 1 in 2.

    Keep it, or trade it in

    Kept babies follow you around, earn Hearts for hats and can become parents themselves: F1, F2, F3. Or the Sanctuary takes one for its fixed value, 0.5 to 6 RF (simulated).

    However deep the line, tap any row of a baby and it traces back to the real Friend, token ID and all, whose pixels it is.

    Your nursery

    One room, four stations. Walk up to one, or tap it. Pick a number to see what it does.

    DNA lab

    Pick any two Friends, lock the rows you want, and hatch an egg with the game's own genetics and hatch animation. Then tap a row of the baby to trace it to its real Friend. A demo: nothing is kept and no RF moves. In the game, parent A is always your own Friend.

    Gene Lab

    The ledger rolls the rarity, you design the genes: RF buys the roll, Hearts buy the genes.

    Free here. In the game a locked row costs 2 Hearts (the first 3 are free), never RF, and the tier still comes from the roll alone.

    Make the dream child

    In the game, your Friend has a daily dream child and a hint: the mate's family. Find that mate, then use the Gene Lab to copy the dream. Each hatch with the dream pair shows how many of its 16 rows match.

    Match all 16 to earn 50 Hearts once per dream and the Dreamchild title. Progress and rewards last for the session; egg tiers and odds stay the same.

    The dream challenge is in the game. This page's lab is a free demo with no Hearts.
